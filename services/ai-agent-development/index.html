FAQ

Common questions about custom AI agents

How much does it cost to build an AI agent?

It depends on scope. Our engagements are anchored to senior engineering day rates from £850 per day, and we quote fixed prices after a short scoping call. A single well-defined workflow costs far less than a multi-agent platform, so we always start by narrowing the problem to the workflow with the clearest return.

How long does it take to develop and deploy an AI agent?

A focused agent for one well-understood workflow typically takes a few weeks, including evaluation and a supervised parallel run. Multi-agent systems with several integrations take longer. We agree the timeline during scoping and ship in stages, so you see working software early rather than waiting for a big launch.

What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions in a conversation. An AI agent takes actions: it reads your systems through APIs, reasons about what to do next, uses tools and completes multi-step tasks such as triaging a ticket or raising a code change. Agents need engineering around them, including permissions, guardrails and monitoring, and that is where most of the real work lies.

Are AI agents safe to use with company data?

They can be, if they are engineered that way. We restrict each agent to the minimum data and permissions it needs, log every action, and keep humans in the loop for consequential steps; in our production systems, code changes are raised as pull requests for human approval. Where data cannot leave your estate, we deploy self-hosted models in your cloud or a private AI cloud. We have taken platforms through ISO 27001 audit twice, and passed both times.

Which AI model should we use: Claude, GPT, Gemini or self-hosted?

We are provider-agnostic. Frontier models such as Claude, GPT and Gemini are usually the fastest route to quality, while self-hosted models served with vLLM, Ollama or llama.cpp suit strict data-residency or cost constraints. We benchmark options against your actual workload during scoping, and we design agents so the model can be swapped later without a rebuild.

Can AI agents handle customer support on their own?

Partly, and that is usually the right goal. Agents are strong at triage, tagging, drafting replies and answering routine questions, which removes a large share of the queue. We design them to escalate anything ambiguous or sensitive to your team, so quality stays high while response times fall. Full autonomy is something you grant gradually, based on measured accuracy, not on day one.

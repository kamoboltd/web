FAQ

Common questions about hiring a SaaS development company

How much does it cost to build a SaaS product?

It depends on scope rather than a rate card. Our senior engineering is anchored at £850 per day, and we quote fixed prices after a short scoping call once we understand the workflows, integrations and compliance requirements involved. A focused MVP needs far less than a full multi-tenant platform, so we help you trim scope to the smallest product that can prove demand before you commit to the bigger build.

How long does it take to build a SaaS MVP?

A tightly scoped MVP is typically a matter of weeks rather than months: one core workflow, one user type, authentication, billing and a deployment pipeline. Timelines stretch when scope creeps, so we agree what is out of the first release as explicitly as what is in. Complex integrations or regulated data add time, and we say so at scoping rather than halfway through the build.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You do. The code, infrastructure definitions and documentation we produce for you are assigned to you, held in your own source control and cloud accounts from the start. There is no lock-in: any competent team can pick up the repositories, the Terraform and the runbooks and carry on without us.

What tech stack do you use for SaaS development?

Our core stack is TypeScript and React on the front end, Node.js or Python services behind an API-first design, PostgreSQL for data, and infrastructure as code with Terraform on Azure, or lean clouds such as DigitalOcean and Hetzner. Where AI features earn their place we embed them, using frontier models or self-hosted ones. We choose proven technology your future hires can maintain over whatever is fashionable this quarter.

Should I hire a SaaS development agency or a freelancer?

A freelancer suits a prototype, and an in-house team makes sense once you have product-market fit and the revenue to fund it. In between, a small senior consultancy usually fits best: you get architecture, build, cloud infrastructure and delivery discipline in one engagement, priced at a day rate rather than big-agency overheads. We work on contract, fixed-price and retained terms, whichever suits the stage you are at.

Can you take over an existing codebase or rescue a stalled project?

Yes. We reverse-engineer entire platforms, including systems we did not build, to find what is actually wrong: storage on slow disks, under-specified message queues, ingress misconfigured for the traffic it faces, or over-provisioned resources quietly wasting money. We stabilise first, then refactor incrementally, so the product keeps serving users while it improves.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes. Many clients move to a managed plan after handover: Application Care starts at £595 per month for monitoring plus one senior day and rises to £3,450 per month for six days and a quarterly roadmap, with a three-month minimum and 30 days' notice. If you would rather run it in-house, we hand over documentation, runbooks and a full walkthrough instead.

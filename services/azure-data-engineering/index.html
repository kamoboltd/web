FAQ

Common questions about Azure data platforms

Is Azure Synapse Link for Dataverse being retired?

Not the service, but its Delta Lake (Parquet) export is: closed to new customers from 15 October 2026 and removed in December 2027. We have moved a production Dataverse estate to Fabric Link and run it as a fixed-scope migration. What is being retired, and how to choose between Fabric Link and Synapse Link CSV.

What does an Azure data engineering consultancy actually do?

We design and build the infrastructure that moves, stores and serves your data on Azure: ingestion pipelines in Data Factory, warehouses and lakehouses in Synapse, Databricks or Microsoft Fabric, and the networking, security and CI/CD around them. The result is data your analysts and applications can rely on, engineered as a platform rather than a pile of one-off scripts.

Should we choose Azure Synapse, Databricks or Microsoft Fabric?

It depends on your workloads and your team. Synapse remains solid for SQL-centric warehousing, Databricks leads for heavy Spark, streaming and machine learning, and Fabric suits teams that want a unified, capacity-billed platform close to Power BI. We resell none of them, so our recommendation is based on your data volumes, skills and budget, not a partner margin.

How long does it take to build a data platform on Azure?

A first production increment usually takes weeks, not years. We stand up the platform foundations and one high-value pipeline first, then extend source by source. A focused ingestion-and-warehouse build is often reporting on real data within the first few weeks; a full enterprise estate is a phased programme that we scope stage by stage.

How much does an Azure data engineering consultant cost in the UK?

Our engagements are anchored on senior day rates from £850 per day, with fixed prices quoted after a scoping call. We work on contract, fixed-price and retained terms, so you can start with a short architecture review before committing to a full build.

Can you integrate Dynamics 365 and Dataverse with our data platform?

Yes. We have built enterprise platforms that feed Dataverse and Dynamics 365 data into Synapse-based warehouses using Synapse Link and Data Factory, landing CRM and ERP data alongside your other sources so reporting comes from one governed model.

Can you connect on-premises databases and legacy systems to Azure?

Yes. We deploy self-hosted integration runtimes so Data Factory and Synapse pipelines can reach databases behind your firewall without exposing them to the internet, and we pair them with private endpoints and hub-spoke networking on the Azure side.

Do you support the data platform after the build?

Yes. Every build ends with documentation, runbooks and a handover to your team. If you want us to stay on, our DataOps managed plan starts at £595 per month for monitoring plus one senior engineering day and scales to £3,450 per month for six days and a quarterly roadmap, with a three-month minimum.

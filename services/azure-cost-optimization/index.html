FAQ

Common questions about Azure cost optimisation services

How much can we realistically save on our Azure bill?

It depends on the estate, which is why we assess before we quote. Across two recent engagements we removed £150k a year from one client's Azure bill and identified £250k a year at another. Common wins are unused resources, over-provisioned VMs, missing reservations, unused Hybrid Benefit and storage in the wrong access tier. The free initial assessment tells you what your realistic range looks like before you spend anything.

Why is my Azure bill so high?

Azure pricing is consumption-based and granular, so small inefficiencies compound quietly: VMs sized for peak load and left there, premium disks on idle machines, orphaned disks and public IP addresses, expired reservations, and machines shut down in the operating system but never deallocated. An audit breaks the bill down by subscription, service and environment so you can see exactly where the money goes.

Should we buy Azure Reserved Instances or a Savings Plan?

Usually both, in layers. Reserved Instances give the deepest discount on a stable, predictable core of virtual machines, while a Savings Plan covers flexible compute that may change size, family or region. Neither covers storage or bandwidth, so commitments are only part of the answer. We model your actual usage before recommending either, because a bad reservation is just a different kind of waste.

What is Azure FinOps and do we need it?

FinOps is the practice of making cloud spend visible, accountable and continuously optimised. You do not need a large programme to benefit: resource tagging, budgets, alerts and a monthly review deliver most of the value. We put those foundations in during an engagement and can run them for you afterwards under a managed plan.

How does the free initial assessment work?

You grant us read-only access to your billing data, or share a cost export. We review spend, usage and configuration, then present our findings: what we would change, roughly what each change is worth, and a fixed price for the work. There is no charge for the assessment and no obligation to go further.

Will cutting Azure costs hurt performance or reliability?

Not when it is engineering-led. Every recommendation is based on measured utilisation rather than guesswork, and we have fixed as many under-provisioned systems as over-provisioned ones. Changes go through your normal review process, so nothing is applied to production unseen.

Do you only work with UK companies?

We are a UK consultancy and most of our clients are UK-based, but we work remotely worldwide. Engagements run over video calls and inside your own Azure tenant, so nothing sensitive leaves your environment.

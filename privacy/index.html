Privacy notice

Last updated 4 November 2026. Kamobo Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described here.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

There is no tracking, no advertising, no tag manager, no session recording and no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to. We do not know who visits this site or how many of you there are.

What is unavoidably processed when you load a page

Three third parties see a request when your browser fetches this site, and each of them necessarily sees your IP address in order to return a response.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. provides the CDN and DNS in front of the site, and processes request data for delivery and security. Cloudflare is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • DigitalOcean, LLC hosts the static files, in their London region.
  • Google LLC serves the three typefaces used here, from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. If you would rather Google did not see that request, a content blocker will stop it and the site will fall back to your system fonts and remain entirely usable.

We do not receive, store or have access to server logs from any of these.

If you email us

Every contact route on this site is a plain mailto: link. There is no form, so nothing is submitted to us through the website itself.

When you email us we hold your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell us, so that we can reply and, if it goes further, so that we can quote for and deliver work. The lawful basis is legitimate interests before a contract exists, and performance of a contract afterwards. We keep enquiry correspondence for up to two years, and records relating to engagements for seven years, which is the period HMRC requires for company records.

Client data during an engagement

In most engagements Kamobo is a data processor rather than a controller, acting on your written instructions under your own contract and data processing terms. We are happy to sign your DPA, and will tell you plainly if anything in it is something we cannot honour.

Where we are given access to a client estate we ask for the least access that will do the job, and for read-only access wherever read-only is enough. The Azure Review is read-only throughout.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to our processing it, or ask us to restrict it. Write to hello@kamobo.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

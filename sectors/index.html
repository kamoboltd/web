Where this work has already been done

Clients are described rather than named. Every engagement below was delivered by the same engineer who would lead yours, and we will go through any of it in detail on a call.

Financial services

For a UK financial services firm, £250,000 a year of Azure spend identified through zombie-resource removal, VM restructuring and reserved instances, with 15+ Azure Workbooks built for cost and performance visibility.

Energy and utilities

For a European energy supplier, Synapse workspaces with self-hosted integration runtimes, Data Factory orchestration and Databricks across dev through production, in centralised Terraform modules with least-privilege networking throughout.

Professional education and training

For a global professional education body, a zero-trust private LLM and RAG platform on GPU-accelerated Azure, where each use case runs on its own isolated vector store so data never crosses between them.

Media and streaming

On a self-hosted media and video-streaming platform we did not build, the architecture was reverse-engineered, load tested with K6 and moved to autoscaling Azure. The cost of running it fell 70% over three years.

Legal and professional services

For a 250-staff UK law firm, three VMware environments audited out of hours, sized and consolidated onto a single Nutanix AHV cluster alongside Azure services.

Employment and public services

For a UK employment-services provider, Kubernetes and Docker Enterprise across five environments, 23 GitLab CI/CD projects, and 35 to 40% taken off the Azure bill.

What these estates have in common

The sector matters less than the shape of the problem.

01

Nobody left can explain it

The people who built it have gone, the documentation is stale, and the risk of touching it is unknown. Reverse-engineering an estate is a large part of this work.

02

The bill grew quietly

Cloud spend rarely spikes. It accretes, through orphaned resources, over-provisioning and instances nobody owns. An audit finds it; the people who own it tell you what is safe to remove.

03

Data cannot leave

Regulated estates cannot send documents to a third-party inference API. That constraint drives the whole architecture, and it is one we have already built against.

04

It has to keep running

None of this happens on a greenfield site. The platform is live, it has users, and the work has to land without an outage.

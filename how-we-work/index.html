How we work

Four things you can hold us to

01

You get the senior person

The engineer who scopes your work is the engineer who does it. Nothing is sold by one person and delivered by another you have never met.

02

Everything as code, everything yours

Infrastructure, pipelines and configuration live in your version control, documented. If you stop working with us tomorrow, your team can still run it.

03

People approve, agents propose

Every AI system we ship has guardrails, an audit trail and a human on the merge button. We will not put something in your estate that acts without being asked.

04

We tell you when it is not worth it

If two days would fix what you were going to spend two months on, we will say so. If we are the wrong people, we will say that too.

Who this suits

The list of what we are not good at lives on the front page, where it can do its job before you have read anything else.

You should talk to us if

  • You want a named engineer who stays on the work, not a team that rotates
  • You have inherited something and nobody left can explain how it works
  • Your cloud bill is growing and the answer keeps being "it is complicated"
  • You want to own the Terraform afterwards rather than rent it from a supplier
  • You need AI in the business but the data cannot leave your tenancy
  • You want someone who will tell you when two days would do instead of two months

Tell us what is actually going wrong.

One email, a couple of paragraphs. We will come back with whether we can help, roughly what it would take and what it would cost. If we are not the right people for it we will say so, and point you at who is.

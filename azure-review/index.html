The Azure Review

Three days, a fixed price, and a written answer to the question you cannot currently answer: what is actually wrong with this estate, what is it costing, and what we would fix first.

Price
£2,500
Duration
3 working days
Access
Read-only
Output
Written findings

Most people who call us already suspect something: the bill is climbing, the deployments are fragile, the security score is stuck, or the person who built it has gone. What they do not have is evidence. Three days of read-only access is usually enough to turn a suspicion into a ranked list with numbers against it.

What you get

  • A full review of the estate: architecture, networking, identity and access, policy and governance
  • Cost analysis with the specific savings available and what each one is worth a year
  • Security and resilience findings, ranked by what would actually hurt
  • A prioritised list of fixes, each sized in days, so you can choose what to do and who does it
  • A walkthrough call to argue with the findings before they are final

What is not included

  • Implementing the fixes. That is a separate piece of work, scoped from the findings
  • Changes of any kind during the review. Access is read-only and stays that way
  • Procurement advice, licence resale or anything we would earn a margin on

The fee comes off the build. If you decide to have us do the work and we start within 30 days, the £2,500 is credited in full against it. If you take the report to your own team or another supplier instead, that is a perfectly good outcome and the report is yours either way.

If your question is only about cost, you do not need this. Cost optimisation starts with a free assessment of your billing data, and that is the cheaper place to start. The Azure Review is for when you also want architecture, security and operational risk looked at.

Before any of it, a free 45-minute call to work out whether a review is even the right thing. Sometimes it obviously is not, and we will say so.

About

The person who scopes it is the person who builds it

Kamobo is led by James Dettmer, and James does the work.

Eighteen years in infrastructure, cloud and platform engineering: data centre and VMware estates first, then Azure, Terraform and DevOps, and for the last few years AI platforms on top of them. Most of that time has been spent inside other organisations' estates as the senior engineer brought in when something needed building properly, or when something built badly had started to cost money.

Where a piece of work genuinely needs more than one pair of hands, we bring in associates we have delivered with before, and we name them before they start. What we do not do is sell you a senior engineer and staff the work with somebody else.

He also builds and runs Kamobo's own software, which is the honest test of any advice we give: GrassrootsDNA is a multi-tenant SaaS that real clubs depend on, and the AI agents we build for clients are the same ones that keep our own platforms running.

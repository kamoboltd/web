About

Kamobo Ltd

Kamobo is a UK software company that designs, builds and runs SaaS platforms, application back-ends and APIs — and the cloud infrastructure they depend on.

We work across the whole spectrum: enterprise workloads on Azure and AWS where governance, security and scale matter, and lean, fixed-cost stacks on DigitalOcean, Hetzner and smaller providers for startups and cost-sensitive teams. Our own products, like GrassrootsDNA, prove the model end to end.

The through-line is simple: engineering that's genuinely useful, priced fairly, and built to last.

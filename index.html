01

Cost tools cannot tell you what nobody needs

An audit tells you what is running and what it costs. It cannot tell you whether anyone still needs it, and that is where the rest of the money is.

02

Load test before you re-architect

Over-provisioned components hide the real limit as effectively as under-provisioned ones. Until you have measured, you are guessing which.

03

Taking over a system nobody can explain

Inheriting a platform is a research job first and an engineering job second. Budget for the reading.

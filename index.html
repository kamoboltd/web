What we are good at, and what we are not

You are going to find this out eventually, so you may as well find it out now. Being told "yes, we do that too" by everyone is how projects go wrong.

Hire us for

Eighteen years of it, most of it hands on keyboard rather than in a governance forum.

  • Microsoft Azure, deep and current
  • Terraform and Ansible across cloud and on-premise
  • Taking over systems you did not build
  • Cutting cloud spend without breaking anything
  • AI agents and private LLM platforms that actually run
  • Azure data platforms on Synapse and Databricks

Where we are not the right call

We would rather lose the enquiry than learn on your budget.

  • AWS-first builds. We have run AWS alongside Azure, but Azure is where the depth is
  • GCP, BigQuery and dbt. Our data record is Azure
  • LangChain and LangGraph. Our agents are built on the Claude SDK and queues
  • VMware VCF 9 and stretched vSAN design. Our VMware work is older than that
  • Pure data science. We build the platform, you bring the models

If what you need is on the right-hand list, say so and we will tell you who to ask instead. Either way, it is a short conversation.
